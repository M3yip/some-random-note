### What are the major type of energy resources?

Energy is required for daily life and activties

There are 2 main types of energys, they are:

* Renewable energy
* Non-renewable energy

**Renewable energy will not run out while non-renewable wont

### Examples of renewable energy

+ Hydroelectric power(HEP)
+ Wave power
+ Tidal power
+ Fuelwood(Biomass)
+ Biofuel(Biomass)
+ Geothermal power
+ Wind power
+ Solar power

### Examples of non-renewable energy

+ Coal
+ Natural gas
+ Oil(Petroleum)

**All these above can also be called "Fossil fuels"(化石燃料)**

+ Nuclear power(Uranium)

### Why fossil fuel are most used energy in the world?

+ Easy and cheap to mine
+ High effience
+ Can be transport to a place and keep low cost
+ Covenient to use

### Is the distribution of fossil fuels is even?

No, the distribution of fossil fuels is uneven.
Different types of fossil fuels distribute more in some place
> _Coal is mainly found in Asia Pacific russia and the USA_  
> _Oil and natural gas are heavily concentrated in the Middle east. Russia and NA are important reserves too_
